The studio

A neighbourhood salon with a bridal heart.

VIOLET sits at Michel Junction on the Krishnapuram–Mavelikkara Road, in Old Mavelikkara. It is a unisex beauty care centre and bridal makeup studio — the kind of place where a bride's trial run matters as much as a Tuesday afternoon haircut.

Guests keep coming back for the same reasons the reviews mention again and again: a calm, well-kept space, staff who take their time, and pricing that stays reasonable for the quality on offer. The studio is women-owned and explicitly LGBTQ+ friendly — everyone is welcome in the chair.
